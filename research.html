Meteoroid dynamics · MSc thesis

Dynamically characterizing sporadic meteoroid origins

Using the REBOUND/x N-body framework, I simulated orbital histories for 387 observed meteors — incorporating radiation pressure alongside gravitational forces — to classify each as asteroidal or cometary in origin. The observations come from the Canadian Automated Meteor Observatory and electron-multiplying CCD cameras.

  • Derived probability distributions for meteoroid orbits at varying ejection times.
  • Focused on velocities below 35 km/s, where distinguishing asteroidal from cometary origins is hardest.
  • Presented as an oral contribution at Meteoroids 2025 in Perth, Australia.

Space situational awareness

Luciole low Earth orbit satellite monitoring

I built a public-facing scientific dashboard that delivers up-to-date information about megaconstellation satellites visible over Canada, supporting space situational awareness.

  • Home — last night's network-wide overview: detection counts, average brightness, and an interactive map of detected ground tracks.
  • Database & Drilldown — in-depth analysis with customizable graphs and tables, filtered and sorted through a simple UI.

Planetary defense · BSc honours thesis

Yarkovsky-effect modification & the close-approach structure of 99942 Apophis

For my honours thesis I ran high-fidelity REBOUND/x simulations across a range of post-2029-flyby scenarios, examining how Earth could alter Apophis's spin state during its 2029 close approach — then recomputed the gravitational "keyholes" with those adjusted spin states.

  • Wrote and successfully defended both the proposal and the thesis paper.
  • Top-10 finalist in the UBC Undergraduate 3 Minute Thesis competition.
  • Presented at the Apophis T-7 Years planetary-defense conference.

Radio astronomy · Co-op project

New radio observations of the supernova remnant CTA 1

I pre-processed and processed observational data from the DRAO Synthesis Telescope to study the supernova remnant CTA 1 using radio continuum, linear polarization, and neutral hydrogen observations. The work was published in The Astrophysical Journal in 2024.

  • Gave two talks — one on the data pre-processing/processing pipeline, one on the CTA 1 analysis.
  • Co-authored with three supervisors at DRAO.
