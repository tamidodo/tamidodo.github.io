PhD candidate · Astronomy

Tammy Do

University of Western Ontario · Western Meteor Physics Group

I study the dynamical origins of meteoroids — working out whether the small bodies that strike Earth's atmosphere came from asteroids or comets by reconstructing their orbital evolution histories. My work spans low-velocity Earth impactors, satellite and debris re-entry detection, and space situational awareness.

Before Western, I completed a BSc in Honours Physics and Astronomy at the University of British Columbia, with research in planetary defense and radio astronomy.

Latest research

Meteoroid dynamics

Dynamically characterizing sporadic meteoroid origins

Simulated orbital histories for 387 observed meteors to classify them as asteroidal or cometary, focusing on the difficult sub-35 km/s velocity regime.

Planetary defense

Yarkovsky modification & the 99942 Apophis close approach

Ran high-fidelity REBOUND/x simulations of Apophis's 2029 flyby to examine Earth's effect on its spin state and recompute gravitational keyholes.
