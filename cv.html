Research Projects

MSc thesis

Characterizing sporadic meteoroid dynamical origins

Simulated orbital histories for meteors observed with the Canadian Automated Meteor Observatory (CAMO) and Electron-Multiplied CCD (EMCCD) cameras owned by the University of Western Ontario. N-body integrations done with REBOUND/REBOUNDX to include both gravitational and radiation pressure forces for higher fidelity. Validated and applied existing orbit-based classification schemes to statistically classify the low-velocity sporadic meteoroid population as either asteroidal or cometary, binned by entry velocity and ablative strength. Possible modifications to the Yarkovsky effect strength after the 2029 close approach of 99942 Apophis
